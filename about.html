About / Scientific profile

Dogus M. Altintas, PhD

Molecular biologist investigating translational control, cancer adaptation and invasive growth.

Scientific trajectory

Following regulation across scales.

My publication record spans androgen signaling and prostate biology, yeast DNA replication and DNA damage, metabolic aging, and MET oncogene regulation. My recent work focuses on how stress-responsive translation and 5′UTR architecture shape cancer cell behavior.

The next step is to develop an independent programme connecting these molecular mechanisms to the persistence and resolution of adaptive cellular states.
